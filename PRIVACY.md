# Moliboard Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-04-20*

Moliboard is a macOS app designed for small children. Keeping kids and families safe is the whole reason this policy is short and blunt.

## What Moliboard collects

**Nothing.**

- No personal information, no names, no email addresses, no device identifiers.
- No analytics, no crash reports, no telemetry.
- No microphone access. Moliboard never listens to audio — it only generates it.
- No camera access.
- No location data.
- No advertising identifiers. There are no ads in the app.

## What Moliboard sends over the internet

**Nothing.**

Moliboard has no network access. It runs entirely on your Mac, offline. You can safely use it on a machine that has never been connected to the internet.

## What Moliboard stores on your device

A tiny amount of preference data is kept in the standard macOS user-defaults store so the app can remember your choices between launches:

- The last background theme you picked (Ocean or Space).
- The last song you played and its progress (so the next time you open the app, you pick up where you left off).
- The currently-selected keyboard layout.

This data never leaves your Mac. Deleting the app removes it.

## Children's privacy

Moliboard is intended for use by small children, but because the app collects no data and transmits nothing, it does not fall under the regulated categories of COPPA, GDPR-K, or similar child-data regimes. There is simply no child data to protect, because none is ever collected.

## Third parties

Moliboard has no third-party SDKs, no third-party analytics, no third-party advertising, and no server backend. The only code that runs is the app itself and the audio sampler that ships with macOS.

## Changes to this policy

If Moliboard ever gains a feature that changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that feature ships, and the "Last updated" date at the top will change.
